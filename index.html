AI Dials support

Get your line connected, troubleshoot a call or manage your call history.

Android

Set up regular calls

Open AI Dials and follow the prompt to set it as your default phone app. Allow phone calls and notifications when prompted. For missed call alerts, check your notification settings and the full-screen alert setting if your phone shows one.

Try AI Practice

Open the menu in AI Dials, choose Practice call and pick a mode. The practice conversation uses sample caller lines so you can try the controls and see the outcome.

Connect the voice bridge

Live AI assistance requires Android 14 or later and an AI Dials bridge on a Linux computer with Bluetooth hands-free support. Pair your phone with the bridge. In Copilot settings, enter the bridge address, Bluetooth device name, access token and certificate fingerprint supplied by the bridge operator. Tap Test and save bridge, then select the paired bridge as the in-call audio device.

If audio returns to the handset, check the selected call audio device. Use Take over to continue the call yourself.

iPhone

Connect an internet calling line

In AI Dials, open Settings. Enter the HTTPS address, line number and access token supplied by your relay operator. Tap Save and connect and wait until the keypad shows that the line is connected.

Place a call or fix a connection

To reach another registered line on the same relay, enter its number or extension and tap Call. For a regular cellular call, tap Use Phone to send the number to the iPhone’s Phone app. If the connection fails, check the relay address and local network access. Ask the relay operator to confirm your line and access token.

Use AI during an internet call

Choose a mode on the call screen. Allow microphone and speech recognition access when prompted. If AI assistance is unavailable, check AI Dials permissions and whether your iPhone supports on-device speech recognition for your language. Ask the relay operator to confirm that the model is running.

Clear call information

On Android, open AI call history to delete a report or clear all reports. Turn off Keep reports on this device in AI settings to remove reports saved across restarts.

On iPhone, use Clear recents to remove recent internet calls and Clear outcome to remove the last transcript and summary.
